## General Rules Applied Above

1. **Start with a strong past-tense verb** — Built, Designed, Led, Engineered, Migrated, Improved, Automated
2. **Name the technology** — Recruiters ctrl+F for stack keywords
3. **End with impact** — A number, a before/after, a user count, a time saved
4. **Avoid "responsible for" and "helped with"** — Own the action
5. **Keep each bullet to one line** — if it wraps to three lines, split it
